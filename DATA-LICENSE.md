# Data licence

The **data produced by FutureTech Radar** — technology records, development records, extracted claims and their quotes, maturity assessments, importance ratings, relationship edges, and the source registry — is licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** licence.

Full legal text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

## What this covers

- Records and fields created by this project (including AI-generated summaries and human-curated assessments).
- Exports of those records (CSV/JSON) published by this project.

## What this does not cover

- **Source documents** (papers, preprints, press releases, articles) are not part of the dataset. They remain under their original licences and are referenced by link, identifier, and short verbatim quotation for the purpose of citation.
- **Upstream metadata** is used under its own licence and remains so: arXiv metadata (CC0 1.0), OpenAlex data (CC0 1.0), ROR data (CC0 1.0). Attribution is given on every record as a matter of practice.
- **Code** in this repository is licensed separately under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`).
